The Ledger starts each item line with its status (`✓ done`, `▸ installing`, `· queued`, `✗ failed`, `– skipped`), all lowercase, in one set of columns. A Machine line ends with a colored summary of its items (`1 of 4 done`, `4 of 4 done`, `1 failed, 1 skipped`, `up to date`) instead of its own status, and a blank line separates Machines.
